# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and instructors publish them.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to have their money back for a Purchase, giving a reason. It is Requested until a person reviews it, then Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, chargeback, reversal

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner when a Refund Request is Approved. It also ends the learner's access to the course from that Purchase.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, chargeback, reversal

**Refund Window**:
The period after a Purchase during which the learner can make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period, "the window"

**Refundable**:
Describes a Purchase that is still inside its Refund Window and whose course the learner has not consumed too much of. Only a Refundable Purchase can have a Refund Request raised.
_Avoid_: Cancellable, eligible for cancellation

**Chargeback**:
A dispute raised by the learner's bank or card issuer that pulls money back without going through a Refund Request. This is a different concept from a Refund.
_Avoid_: Using it as a synonym for Refund or Refund Request
